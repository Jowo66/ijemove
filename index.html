FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

We answered them so you don't have to ask.

How do I request a ride?
Once you've created an account with your details and payment information, set your pick-up and drop-off locations, choose your ride type, review the estimated fare, and request a ride. When the trip is complete the app handles payment, lets you rate your driver, and sends a digital receipt.
How much do I pay to order a ride?
The cost of a trip is fixed — what is displayed is what you pay. To see the cost, simply enter your pick-up and drop-off locations in the app and you'll be given a price.
What if I want a dedicated ride?
Ije offers a chauffeur service that lets you book our drivers for a minimum of 2 hours and a maximum of 24 hours. Free cancellation up to 2 hours before the booked time.
What happens if I need to cancel a ride?
You can cancel at any time prior to the driver's arrival. A cancellation fee is charged if the driver is within 5 minutes of your pick-up point.
What should I do if I left something in the vehicle?
Contact us at customerservice@ijemove.com as soon as possible. Drivers return lost items to our local office or report them to the police. Bring valid photo ID to the local office to retrieve your item. Lost items are held for 28 days, after which they are disposed of.
Do you have any discounts or promo codes available?
Stay connected for future offers and discounts. Check the app or website for the latest deals on your rides so you don't miss out on savings.
Is Ije hiring?
Our hiring situation changes over time. Check the job listings on our website or reach out to info@ijemove.com for more information.
